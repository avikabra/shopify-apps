## Long description
AI shopping agents are already sending merchants traffic — and buying the wrong products when catalog data is thin.

Agent-Ready Catalog Fixer makes every product buyable by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode:

- **0–100 agent-readiness score** — every product graded A–F on the six things agents check: GTIN/barcode validity, description depth, structured data, image alt text, variant completeness, and Catalog eligibility.
- **One-click auto-fixes** — assign valid barcodes, expand thin descriptions into attribute-rich copy, draft FAQs, fill image alt text, and publish Product + FAQPage JSON-LD.
- **Catalog rejection radar** — flags the missing brand, GTIN, or description issues that get products silently rejected from Shopify Catalog, with exact fix steps.
- **Continuous monitoring** — products are re-scored automatically whenever you update them, so your catalog never drifts.

Built for the agentic era: agents compare on verifiable facts, not ad spend. Good data lets small brands win recommendations.

## FAQ (draft)
**Do the auto-generated GTINs work?** They're syntactically valid placeholders so agents can verify your products today. Replace them with your GS1-issued GTINs before publishing to sales channels — the app labels them clearly.
**Will this change my storefront?** No. Fixes update product data and metafields; the JSON-LD snippet is yours to add to your theme when ready.
**What happens when I uninstall?** Your audit data is deleted per Shopify's privacy requirements.
