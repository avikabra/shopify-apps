# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how our Shopify apps ("Simple Purchase Orders & Stock Alerts" and "Simple Returns & Exchanges", collectively "the Apps") collect, use, and protect information when you install and use them.

**Contact:** av3kabra@gmail.com

## Information We Collect

### Simple Purchase Orders & Stock Alerts
- **Shop information:** Shop domain, shop name, and access tokens (encrypted) required for app functionality.
- **Supplier data:** Supplier names, contact details, and product-SKU mappings you enter.
- **Inventory data:** Product information, stock levels, purchase orders, and transfer records from your Shopify store.
- **No customer personal data** is collected or stored by this app.

### Simple Returns & Exchanges
- **Shop information:** Shop domain, shop name, and access tokens (encrypted) required for app functionality.
- **Order data:** Order names/numbers for returns processing.
- **Customer data:** Customer email addresses, names (when provided), and Shopify customer IDs — collected solely to process return requests and send status notifications.
- **Return data:** Return request details, reasons, statuses, and related communications.

## How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Provide the app's core functionality (purchase orders, returns processing)
- Send notifications you've configured (low-stock alerts, return status emails)
- Improve app performance and fix issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Storage and Security

- Access tokens are encrypted using industry-standard encryption (Fernet/AES).
- Data is stored on secure servers (Railway, US region).
- We implement reasonable technical measures to protect against unauthorized access.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- When you uninstall an app, all associated data is deleted within 48 hours per Shopify's requirements.
- You may request data deletion at any time by contacting av3kabra@gmail.com.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the data we hold about your shop
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent (by uninstalling the app)

Contact av3kabra@gmail.com for any privacy-related requests.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the app or email.

## Shopify's Role

These apps run on the Shopify platform. Shopify's own privacy practices are governed by [Shopify's Privacy Policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy).
