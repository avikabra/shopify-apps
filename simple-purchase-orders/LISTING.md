## Long description
Shopify retired Stocky. If you miss simple purchase orders, this is for you.

Simple Purchase Orders & Stock Alerts is the no-nonsense inventory companion for small Shopify stores:

- **Suppliers** — keep your supplier list with contact details and link products to the SKUs they provide.
- **Purchase orders** — create POs in seconds, email them to suppliers as PDFs, and receive stock against them (including partial receipts).
- **Low-stock alerts** — set reorder thresholds per product and get a daily email digest of everything running low.
- **One-click reordering** — turn a low-stock alert into a purchase order without retyping anything.
- **Stock transfers** — move inventory between locations with paired adjustments that keep counts honest.
- **Operations dashboard** — open POs, low-stock items, and recent receipts at a glance.

No forecasting models, no ERP complexity, no per-user pricing. Just the purchase orders and stock visibility a small store actually uses.

**Pricing:** flat monthly plans based on how many SKUs you manage — not users, not orders, not revenue.
