## Long description
Returns shouldn't require an enterprise contract.

Simple Returns & Exchanges gives small Shopify stores a clean, branded returns portal that just works:

- **Branded customer portal** — customers request returns and exchanges from a page that matches your store, no login required.
- **Return rules you control** — set return windows, restocking fees, and which products are final sale.
- **Refunds, store credit, or exchanges** — approve a return and choose: refund to the original payment method, issue store credit via gift card, or create an exchange order.
- **Return labels** — generate prepaid return shipping labels right from the dashboard.
- **Status emails** — customers get notified at every step: request received, approved, label ready, refund issued.
- **Merchant dashboard** — see every return, its status, and what action is needed, all in one place.

**Pricing that makes sense for small stores:** flat monthly plans with no per-return fees and no revenue share. You always know what you'll pay.

Built for stores doing up to a few hundred orders a month that want Loop-style returns without the Loop-style contract.

## Pricing plans
- **Starter — $12/month**: Up to 50 returns per month. Customer portal, return rules, refunds, labels, status emails.
- **Growth — $24/month**: Unlimited returns. Everything in Starter, plus exchanges and priority support.
- 14-day free trial on both plans.

## FAQ (draft)
**Do you charge per return?** No. Both plans are flat monthly pricing with no per-return fees.
**Does it work with my theme?** Yes — the portal is a standalone page linked from your store, plus an optional theme extension.
**What happens when I uninstall?** Your return data is deleted per Shopify's privacy requirements.
